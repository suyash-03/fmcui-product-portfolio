A clear space to
think with your Mac.

Chat with Apple Foundation Models in a focused desktop app. Write, explore ideas, and work through code with an on-device model that keeps your prompts on your Mac.

Get fmcui for Mac ↗ Explore the code

A conversation in fmcui, running the on-device model.

Everything in its place.

From the first question to the next idea, the interface stays out of your way.

Private by default

On device is the default model. Your prompts stay on this Mac when you use it, and conversation history is saved locally.

Room for the whole thought

The composer moves to the bottom once a conversation begins, leaving a generous space to read and respond.

Made for useful answers

Streaming replies, readable Markdown, and copyable code blocks help you move from a question to something you can use.

Start with a question.

Open a new chat and get a quiet canvas with a few ideas to get started. Pick up older conversations from the sidebar whenever you need them.

Private Cloud appears only when the Mac's fm server offers it. The app never switches an on-device chat to the cloud on its own.

Ready when you are.

fmcui runs on macOS 27 or later on a Mac with Apple Intelligence available.
